FOUNDER & MANAGING PARTNER

Adnan ChamaaFounder & Managing Partner

At PHARUS, our purpose has always been clear: to provide trusted expertise where experience, judgement and discretion matter most.

Today’s challenges increasingly cross borders and disciplines. Security, technology, financial and corporate structures, and specialist operational requirements demand not only technical knowledge but also sound judgement, trusted relationships and an understanding of the environments in which our clients operate.

PHARUS brings together expertise across security, cyber security, policing and emergency services, military advisory and training, healthcare management, and asset tracing and financial investigations.

We also maintain professional relationships and collaborative links with established international organisations, including TDI in Washington, D.C., together with specialist companies in the United Kingdom, the United States and other markets.

Integrity and confidentiality are not simply values we state; they are fundamental to the way we work.

Our commitment remains to build long-term relationships founded on trust, integrity, discretion and professional excellence.
